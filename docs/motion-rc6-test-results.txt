PASS tests/motion-home-smooth.cjs, collapse-anchor.cjs, motion-coordination.cjs, motion-endpoint.cjs, service-bottom-frames.cjs, transition-frames.cjs, service-resize-ipc.cjs, window-motion.cjs и release-readiness.cjs.
